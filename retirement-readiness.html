Retirement Income: Is a Fixed Annuity Worth a Look?

Annuities aren't right for everyone. Here's plain-English information to help you think it through — plus a simple way to get your specific questions answered.

What is an annuity?

The basics before anything else.

1

How it works

You contribute funds — either a lump sum or over time — and the insurance company agrees to pay you back later, starting now or at a future date you choose.

2

Why people consider one

Predictable retirement income, protection from market downturns, and tax-deferred growth while your money stays in the contract.

3

What it's not

Not a get-rich-quick investment, not a stock or mutual fund, and not built for short-term cash needs. Early withdrawals can trigger surrender charges.

4

Built for the long game

One piece of a retirement plan, not the whole plan — used to help protect principal and provide steady income later.

Types we work with

Insurance-based annuity contracts, not market securities.

MYGA (Fixed Annuity)

Multi-Year Guaranteed Annuity — works a bit like a CD, locking in a fixed interest rate for a set number of years.

FIA (Fixed Index Annuity)

Returns are linked in part to a market index with downside protection on principal — a middle ground between growth potential and safety.

Income Rider

An optional add-on available on some annuities that can provide a guaranteed stream of income starting on a future date you choose, usually for an additional cost.

What we don't offer: Variable annuities are securities products regulated by the SEC and FINRA, requiring a separate securities license. We focus exclusively on fixed and fixed-index annuities — insurance contracts within our license as a Texas General Lines Agent — so what you see here is the full scope of what we can help with.

Who tends to look into this

A fixed or fixed-index annuity is worth exploring if any of this sounds like you. It's not a fit for everyone, and that's okay — the point is just to think it through.

Worth a look if you...

  • Want part of your retirement income locked in and predictable
  • Are rolling over a 401(k) or IRA and want to protect a portion of it
  • Are concerned about outliving your savings
  • Want to reduce exposure to market swings for some of your money

Common questions

Straight answers, no jargon.

Are annuities safe?

Annuities are insurance contracts, not market investments. Fixed and fixed-indexed annuities aren't directly exposed to stock market losses, and payments are backed by the claims-paying ability of the issuing insurance company — they are not bank products and are not FDIC-insured.

Can I lose money in an annuity?

Fixed and fixed-index annuities protect 100% of principal from market loss, though fees, surrender charges, and early withdrawals can still reduce value. Choosing the right type for your goals matters — that's exactly what a conversation is for.

What happens if I die before using it?

Most annuities include a death benefit that passes remaining value to your named beneficiaries, though the details vary by contract. We'll walk through your specific options together.

Is this tax or investment advice?

No — this page is educational only. Annuity guarantees are backed by the issuing insurance company, not the government, and features, rates, and availability vary by carrier, product, and state. Talk to your CPA or financial advisor about how an annuity fits your overall tax and investment picture.
