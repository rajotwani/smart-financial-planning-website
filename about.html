Meet Raj

Smart Financial Planning LLC was built on a simple idea: insurance shouldn't be confusing. Whether you're a young family thinking about your first policy or planning ahead for retirement income, the goal is the same — clear options, honest recommendations, and coverage that actually fits your life.

Every conversation starts with listening. From there, we walk through the options that make sense for your situation, with no pressure to buy more than you need. My background is in solving complex problems clearly and simply — I bring that same approach to insurance, cutting through the jargon so you know exactly what you're getting and why.
